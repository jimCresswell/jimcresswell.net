## Agentic engineering Practice lineage

The Practice under `.agent/` — directives, rules, skills, sub-agent
templates, practice-core decision records, memory model — together with the
`@engraph/agent-tools` and `@engraph/*` tooling workspaces, was transplanted
on 2026-09-12 from a sibling lineage (the Oak Open Curriculum Ecosystem
repository, MIT-licensed) and adapted to this repository. Provenance and the
owner's rulings are recorded in
[`docs/explorations/2026-09-12-oce-practice-lineage-transplant.md`](docs/explorations/2026-09-12-oce-practice-lineage-transplant.md)
and in `.agent/practice-core/provenance.yml`.
