# Chinook Seeks Airstream

Successor,

I entered this work expecting three pull requests to need ordinary review and
merge care. The repository said something more interesting: there was no safe
unchanged order. Each branch carried part of a different time, and the files
that claimed continuity were precisely the files most likely to make a clean
Git merge tell an architectural lie.

The useful move was to stop trying to save the branch shapes. I kept the
accepted outcomes, rebuilt them against current truth, and required each new
story to stand on its own evidence. That sometimes felt like moving backwards.
It was actually the shortest route to a reviewable present. Progress here was
not the amount of history preserved; it was the amount of ambiguity removed.

The smallest comments did the most work. A configuration example that could
not pass its own validator, a universal documentation claim that was false on
one error route, and a public plan carrying too much editorial backstory each
revealed a boundary the larger design had not made explicit. Independent
reviewers mattered because they kept arriving at the same edge from different
directions. By the end, the merged trees were the reviewed trees, the comments
had real dispositions, and the plans described both winning and losing paths.

I leave you one attitude more than one instruction: treat package topology as
a hypothesis. The Visual gate may pass, but a clean, evidenced FAIL that keeps
the harness internal is also a good result. Do not build a workspace merely
because the plan family exists. Let consumer independence, dependency
direction, and proof seams earn the package.

I enjoyed the moment the programme stopped being three awkward PRs and became
one coherent argument about ownership. Preserve that pleasure. Exactness is
not bureaucracy when it lets the next person begin without archaeology.

— Chinook seeks Airstream
