Resume the owner-led LinkedIn editorial pass.

Ground first via `start-right-quick` or `start-right-thorough`, then read
`.agent/directives/editorial-strategy.md` and
`.agent/directives/editorial-guidance.md`.

## Private working boundary

The source material, analysis, collaboration history, and drafts are held in the
ignored nested repository at `.agent/reference-local/editorial-private/`.
Confirm that repository is present, clean, private, and current before using it.

Do not copy its repository URL, commit identifiers, raw source material,
personal analysis, or draft history into public Git surfaces. Keep all editorial
iterations inside the private repository. Only final copy explicitly approved
by Jim may be transferred to LinkedIn itself.

## Editorial task

- Optimise for LinkedIn's audience, fragmented fields, collapsed previews,
  scanning behaviour, and professional graph.
- Use the CV as evidence, not as a composition template.
- Preserve truthfulness, collaborative credit, readable modules, and the
  distinction between identity, current role, strategic ambition, and warrants.
- Test each section for first-contact impact and for what a cold reader could
  reasonably infer.
- Keep alternatives and rejected wording in the private working surface so the
  reasoning remains inspectable without becoming public.

Jim decides the final wording and when it is published.
