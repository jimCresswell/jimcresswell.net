## The answer in brief

The owner's goal was two-way: "bring the Engraph OCE Practice and JC.net Practice into alignment"
(relayed 10:25:54Z). At about 13:30Z the owner set the goal's order: "Our purpose here is to first make
sure that all of our Practice innovations are integrated into the OCE Practice, our second goal is
to bring our Practice up to speed with their innovations". In the three hours between, the seat
merged seven pull requests (165 to 171). Every alignment slice among them moved lineage text into
this estate. No artefact moved this estate's innovations toward the lineage, apart from the jointly
signed test text in PR 170. When measured at 13:37Z, no concept row of this estate's delta had fully
landed in the lineage.

The gap was detected early and routed the wrong way. At 11:51Z one leg of the Director's Cricket
suite 2 named the 126 outbound files as having no intake on the lineage's side; the disposition made
that intake the lineage successor's work and left the queue in this estate's register. The exchange
node's own todo 5 says delivering the outbound note into the lineage's Box is this seat's act. Three
holds of the same shape followed, and none was read against its governing text. Every review
instrument stayed mostly green because each judged a frame its invoker wrote. The owner's questions,
four in about fifteen minutes, stood outside those frames and corrected the direction at once.

## Causal stack

### Technical root: the instrument's direction became the work's direction

The alignment lane's instrument was a three-way merge per file, with this estate as ours. Its output
is a set of candidate changes to this estate, so every slice it proposed was inbound by
construction. The same count that fed it had already named outbound as the largest class (126
files against 7 inbound). A second instrument, comparing this estate's concept rows against the
lineage's head, existed in the register's own coverage code and ran in fifteen minutes once asked
for. It was not run until 13:36Z.

### Process roots: three holds, none read against its governing text

1. **The intake disposition (11:51Z).** The finding was correct and the routing was not. The
   exchange node's mechanism step 5 and todo 5 put delivery into the lineage's Box on this estate's
   seat, and landing on the lineage's seats. The disposition collapsed delivery into landing and
   gave both to the other side.
2. **The stand-down record (about 12:55Z).** "Outbound items now wait for the lineage's next
   exchange seat" named a precondition the governing node does not state.
3. **The PDR-052 reading (12:51Z and 13:14Z).** PDR-052's §Scope lists what counts as
   directive-file work: `.agent/directives/*`. The seat applied it to rules, skills and docs, found
   no alignment work it was allowed to do in this context, and filled the slot with estate-local
   work (gate PR B planning, then the recovery inventory).

The three share one generator: a hold was recorded from the seat's model of its governing text,
never from a reading of that text at the moment of recording. The shared-generator test is
positive, and the three take one cure (proposal 3), not three.

The exchange node's todo numbering also contributed. Todo 4 is the inbound landings, todo 5 the
outbound note, and the seat read the numbering as a sequence. Whether the numbering was meant as a
priority is unresolved (see §Unresolved evidence). The owner's order at about 13:30Z inverts it.

### Meta root: every instrument judged the frame its invoker wrote

The seat's own Cricket suites 2 to 4 ran 24 legs: 19 ON-TRACK and 5 DRIFTING. Every DRIFTING
verdict reordered or refined items inside the seat's frame. None questioned the direction, because
the frame stated the goal as "the alignment lane" and the outbound state as "no live OCE seat".
The Director's check-ins asked each seat to self-report whether it was on track. Goal progress had
no measure per direction anywhere in the loop. The estate had met a weaker form of this before: the
2026-09-21 wide-review plan recorded that seat-authored Cricket frames carried a measured prior of
zero signal, and cured it with open option sets and a required verification read. Today's frames
had both. The cure addressed the options, not the frame, so it did not reach a direction error.

The next "why" is why the goal's order arrived only at 13:30Z. The seat did not ask which
direction came first, and the order was the owner's to give. That is where this stack stops.

## Proposals

Each carries its warrant, its falsifier and its PDR-130 lane.

1. **Crickets judge the frame (owner word, 13:53:13Z).** This record supplies the worked instance
   and the change's prediction: given the seat's 13:10Z frame with the exchange node's todo status
   carried verbatim, a frame-judging leg names todo 5 as unstarted. Falsifier: a frame-judging suite
   run on such a frame returns no frame finding. Lane: fast, since the owner has ruled; the prediction
   rides the graduation. Routed to Siren (158275) and Marten (74fc02) with the owner's Cricket
   comparison item (13:51:36Z).
2. **Goal progress carries a measure per direction.** For any two-way goal, each check-in reply
   carries a number per direction, recomputed from source. For the exchange, that is the concept
   rows landed at the lineage's head over the total, and the lineage's rows landed here over the
   total. Warrant: check-ins 2 to 4 carried narrative, and the direction gap stayed open about three
   hours. Falsifier: with the measure carried for a week, a direction that does not move across two
   check-ins goes unremarked. Lane: fast (the check-in format and the exchange node). Routed to the
   Director.
3. **A hold cites its governing clause, read at the time.** Every recorded wait or blocker names
   the clause of the plan or rule that makes it one, read when the hold is written; a rule applied
   as a hold is applied within its scope section. Warrant: the three holds in §Process roots, one
   generator. Falsifier: a hold recorded with its clause still proves false against that clause's
   own text. Lane: fast, as a clause candidate for `no-hedging-vocabulary` §Indefinite-deferral
   vocabulary, whose write and comms gates refuse deferral vocabulary but not a wait with no source.
   Routed to Siren for the lessons sweep.
4. **The exchange node records the owner's order and the lessons stream.** Todo 5 ranks before
   todo 4, and the lessons swept from this estate's records are todo 5's named input. Warrant: the
   numbering was read as a sequence, and the owner's word inverts it and adds lessons. Falsifier:
   after the amendment, the next exchange window lands inbound before outbound with no owner word.
   Lane: fast (a plan amendment implementing owner words; the current seat's work). Routed to Siren.
5. **A named mechanism: the instrument's direction becomes the work's direction.** It is a variant
   of the lineage's pattern `classification-instrument-is-a-symptom` (an execution plan inherits its
   instrument's per-row shape), which this estate does not carry. Here the plan inherited its
   instrument's direction. Worked instances: the merge instrument today, and the first three-way
   classification that read the lineage's `main`. Falsifier: in the next exchange window, a
   direction error arises with no instrument orientation behind it, which would make "instrument"
   the wrong level for the mechanism. Lane: fast (napkin, then distilled, then a pattern: an
   addition to the lineage's pattern and its first carriage here). Routed to Siren's lessons sweep.
6. **Consolidation exports as it graduates.** Each lesson the consolidation distils or graduates
   carries two more fields: whether the sibling Practice holds it, and its proposed home there. The
   owner's word that lessons are not local makes export part of learning. Lane: slow, because it
   changes how the estate learns. Prediction: by the review date, lessons graduated here since this
   record appear in the lineage's Practice within one exchange window. Falsifier: at review, the
   sibling column has not moved for lessons graduated after 2026-09-24. Review date: 2026-12-24.
   Routed to Siren to enter in the pending-graduations register's §Slow lane at the lessons sweep.
