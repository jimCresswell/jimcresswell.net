## The answer in brief

- **The recorder is the donor.** Landings enter the register only when JC.net's seat writes them.
- **The rows are compound.** One row often holds several concepts that land in separate pull
  requests, or that the receiver declines one by one, and the predicate has no state for a
  declined member.
- **The row set is pinned.** Concepts made since the register's pin get no row until the once-only
  re-pin at the close.
- **The lessons have no rows**, though ruling 36 makes them part of goal one.

The measure was adopted by agreement and never checked against a first-hand read. Three readings
of one state disagreed, and none matched the row texts.

## Causal stack

### Technical root: a hand-applied predicate over compound, pinned, donor-recorded rows

1. **The recorder.** §Landings rows are written in JC.net pull requests by JC.net's seat. The
   lineage's 13 overnight merges could enter only after that seat woke, and they were queued
   behind drafting when it did.
2. **The grain.** A row such as J18 reads "bring the observer (absent there); compare the two
   reference notes and the prompt". Both estates work below the row. The lineage lands, and
   declines, one concept at a time. The donor delivered J18's halves in different batches. So rows
   sit PARTIAL, and each reader rounds a partial row differently. The predicate has no state for a
   member the receiver declined, so J9's four declines hold the row open as surely as undone work.
3. **The pin.** Rows cover this estate's delta since `55649a20e6`. The seat delivered the day's
   freshest innovations first; batches two and three and the K sets carried mostly concepts newer
   than the pin. The owner's goal names all innovations, so this was not wrong. But the count
   could not show those landings at all.
4. **The lessons.** Ruling 36 of the exchange node puts "the lessons swept from this estate's
   records" into todo 5's material. The register has no lesson rows.

### Process roots

1. **Recording queued behind drafting.** The lag was known twice this morning: by the Director at
   10:36Z, and by this seat at 10:53Z when it told the lineage J4 and J18 had landed. The rows
   stayed at queue item 4. Recording a landing costs a prose pull request with its review rounds,
   so it waits for a batch of rows to carry.
2. **Routed is not queued.** The 2026-09-24 retrospective made six proposals and routed five to
   this seat. On taking them that afternoon, the seat sent P3 (a hold cites its clause), P5 (the
   instrument mechanism) and P6 (the consolidation exports) "to the lessons sweep". The sweep
   appears in no queue:
   - not the queue of 16:31Z;
   - not the handover's queue at 18:20Z;
   - not this morning's queue.

   None of the three reached a rule, `distilled.md` or the slow-lane register. The lessons stream
   they were to ride has no artefact after the 13:40Z pilot. The words named a destination; no
   artefact fired.

### Meta root: the measure was adopted by agreement and never calibrated

The count was agreed between the two exchange seats at 14:50Z. Both seats' records carried it, and
so did every check-in reply that quoted it. No one compared it with a first-hand read of a known
state until this record. The first such comparison found three readings that disagreed with each
other and with the row texts. A gauge taken on trust reads its own construction: here, the
donor's recording habit and the row set fixed at the pin.

The next "why" is why goal one is measured on donor-recorded rows pinned once. The register and
its once-only re-pin are ratified structure (the node's todo 7). Changing the pin is the owner's.
Computing a measure beside it, and calibrating it, is not. The stack stops there.
