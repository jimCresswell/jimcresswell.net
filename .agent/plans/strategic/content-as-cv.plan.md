# The content is the CV

## Outcome

The CV at `jimcresswell.net` reads as one person's professional identity, canonical and
current: no variant copy, no claim without a fact behind it, every section written under
the editorial directives. The LinkedIn profile is composed from the same evidence for its
own audience, behind the private boundary, and never staged here.

## User groups and value

- **Readers of the CV** (hiring managers, collaborators, peers) get one truthful account
  that reads well and says what the owner does.
- **The owner** gets a single place where the facts live, so every channel is edited from
  the same ground.
- **Seats on the repository** get closed editorial questions (positioning, capabilities,
  metadata, experience) and an explicit list of doors that stay shut.

## The bet

That one canonical view, held to the editorial directives, is worth more than tailored
variants; ADR-021 retired the tilts on that bet and the re-entry route is preserved should
it prove wrong.

## Success looks like

The editorial questions the legacy corpus settled stay settled; the deferred doors
(CONTENT-3) reopen only through a plan and a decision record; the LinkedIn work completes
behind its boundary with nothing leaking into the public tree.
