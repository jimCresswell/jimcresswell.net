# The platform — the site and the Practice

## Outcome

The site builds, deploys and proves itself at the production layer on every change; its
dependencies are patched and its majors taken one per slice; the Practice runs the shared
lineage's current generation with this repository's divergence recorded; and the learning
loop the Practice exists for (napkin, distilled, pending graduations, doctrine) is closed
here rather than nominal.

## User groups and value

- **The owner** spends attention on the content and the graph, not on the ground they stand
  on, and takes a lineage generation when the delta is worth the rulings.
- **Seats on the repository** meet gates that exist, entry points that describe this host,
  and assertions exercised before they were trusted.
- **The lineage** (and the next host, castr) gets a runbook, instruments and inputs from
  each transplant instance instead of a re-derivation.

## The bet

That stability here is bought with proof and with convergence on the lineage's practice,
not with caution; and that the transplant runbook, run as instruments, makes each later
generation a fraction of the first.

## Success looks like

`pnpm check` green on every leg and CI at parity; the visual and production proof current;
the castr instance of the transplant runbook filling the timing table's second column; the
57 archived lessons synthesised and routed; no assertion in a session-open surface left
unexercised.
