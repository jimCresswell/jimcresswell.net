## How to edit

Start with the intended understanding, actual work and Jim’s voice. Keep the register confident,
serious underneath, and open to humour. Use the [editorial guidance](../.agent/directives/editorial-guidance.md)
and the [research](research/exceptional-cv-linkedin-research-report-2026-09-26.md) to improve the
writing, not to turn each sentence into a governance exercise.

About supplies orientation; Experience explains scope, action and attribution; selected links
let a reader inspect the work. Repetition earns its place only when those jobs differ. Short
engagements can stay short. Optional sections can stay absent. Keep the recorded photo decision
and existing useful material unless a concrete issue justifies reopening them.

Two countertests remain useful:

- Do not impose “generalist” or “rapid learner” as a career-wide explanation. Familiar role and
  field labels can orient readers, while concrete work conveys what those labels miss. Expertise,
  prior knowledge and correction still matter.
- Changing a method or decision is not, by itself, evidence of improved judgement. If a passage
  claims learning or improvement, explain what prompted the change and what improved; consider
  avoidable initial error and process cost as possible alternatives. These are tests of a claim,
  not diagnoses of Jim.

Preserve Jim’s account, documentary observations, interpretation and proposed wording as
different kinds of support. Do not convert intention into delivered benefit, a mechanism into
proven effectiveness, or collaborative and agent work into sole authorship. Conversely, do not
erase genuine origination, delivery or organisational judgement because it lacks a public metric.
