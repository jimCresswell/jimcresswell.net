## Completion decision and useful sequence

At completion on 27 September, the baseline, audit and gap analysis were accepted as a substantive profile assessment with explicit limits. Every discovered profile entry and relevant optional section had a state and disposition. Posts and Comments reached their observed accessible boundaries; reactions reached the revised proportional boundary. Material findings traced to observations, Jim's account or identified sources. A separate AI review challenged omissions, attribution, inference and overcomplication and accepted delivery after the consistency corrections recorded in the [audit methods](profile-audit-2026-09-27.md#methods-appendix-and-review-status). This public edition preserves professional evaluative substance while omitting the sensitive material described at the start.

The original exhaustive ambitions remained only partly achieved for peripheral activity links and third-party multimedia interiors/audio (A02/A04). These prevent claims of a fully deduplicated event dataset or complete audiovisual archive. Format availability and role/degree relationships were checked. The public authwall is an observed access limit; native mobile and external reader testing are untested; standalone screenshots are optional packaging. None is silently presented as absence, and none is a reason to withhold supported findings.

The strongest supported sequence was: **(1)** apply Jim's confirmed Principal Engineer role history and correct other stale facts; **(2)** explain organisational contribution, curriculum infrastructure and the Practice in prominent profile surfaces; **(3)** connect selected examples to direct evidence routes and repair the failed Galaxy Zoo attachment; **(4)** refine earlier roles, research summaries and skill/interest ordering. A new banner, more sections or a posting programme had weaker justification. Contribution explanation need not wait for public proof of every leadership act or publication of underlying research.

This sequence preserves the serious alternative that accurate facts, concrete explanations and a few useful links may deliver most of the benefit. It also corrects the earlier audit's tendency to undersize the substantive work. A [complete first draft](../profile-draft.md) now carries this work forward for discussion. Live implementation and any external reader testing remain separate. The historical audit made no profile changes, publication, repository push or contact with another person.
