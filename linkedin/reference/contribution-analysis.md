28 September 2026 · Repository edition for the LinkedIn workspace

This account carries the professional substance relevant to [the first draft](../profile-draft.md).
It is an editorial synthesis of Jim’s account, the recorded profile, the current website and public
work. It does not measure reader response or the causal impact of an individual within team results.

## Main judgement

The existing LinkedIn profile supplies a recognisable role and a substantial career, but its
opening gives little explanation of the contribution. The website gives more of that account,
including organisational work and research, while some broad claims need narrower wording.
The useful repair is selective explanation: what Jim did, why the decision or mechanism matters,
whose work it connects with and what a reader can inspect.

The record supports engineering creation, organisational contribution and research judgement.
They can reinforce one another without becoming a single compulsory identity. Delivery, quality,
maintenance, teaching, scientific inquiry and human interests also deserve space on their own terms.

## Oak: engineering organisation and sustained delivery

Jim’s account describes helping shape Engineering from four contractors into five product squads
and three platform groups. His contribution includes designing structure with colleagues,
coaching engineers and translating technical choices for non-technical leadership. He also
identifies engineering management, quality assurance, continuous delivery and engineering culture
as parts of the achievement.

The rebuild account supplies a concrete decision: increasing change complexity was threatening
delivery; Jim advocated a deeper intervention and helped manage the immediate risk as it was
taken forward. The relevant contribution includes judgement and responsibility within collective
work. Neither organisational growth nor the platform’s continued development establishes his
isolated causal share. The account need not be diluted into a generic leadership label or depend
on a public commit for every act of coaching.

## Curriculum access and the Practice

The user-facing service brings Oak curriculum resources into AI assistants. Teachers can find
and explore resources while retaining professional authority over what suits their class.
Underneath are reusable curriculum engineering tools: API access, search, curriculum relationships
and evidence resources. They are part of the service account, not its complete explanation.

Jim’s account supports origination, architecture, implementation and end-to-end delivery.
Oak owns the curriculum and service; curriculum specialists created the educational material.
Other engineering contributions and extensive agent assistance remain part of the build.
“Built” expresses delivery responsibility without implying sole authorship of every component.

The Practice is the research, methods and tooling for engineering with AI agents. It includes
reasoning, collaboration, review, knowledge continuity and learning mechanisms. Research direction,
conceptual choices and implementation are contributions; they do not by themselves establish
comparative effectiveness or field-wide novelty. The Innovation Kit is a related strategy for
making possibilities tangible and testable; a strategy is not proof that its portfolio has shipped.

## Science, independent inquiry and human detail

The scientific account should expose an actual question and result. The
[2006 topology paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0512017) tests a proposed explanation for
microwave-sky alignments; the improvement is modest. The
[2009 galaxy-bias paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1101) examines changes with scale, luminosity
and colour. The [thesis](https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/luminosity-functions-and-galaxy-bias-in-the-sloan-digital-sky-sur/)
also investigates distinctions between colour and morphology using Galaxy Zoo classifications.
These support substantive research claims without requiring a claim that the work forced an
entire field to change.

Obaith was research intended to become a startup. Jim chose to stop and reports learning about
the importance of delivery over perfect planning. This is a worthwhile account without inventing
a reason for stopping, a commercial failure, a delivered product or a universal lesson about his
personality. Exact dates remain a local factual question.

Institute of Physics membership is current. Growing Communities volunteering, the allotment,
plant breeding and the Theremin add concrete human detail. They do not need to function as
metaphors for engineering or prove a theory of the whole career.
